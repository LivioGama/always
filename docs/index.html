Speak. It types. Anywhere.

Always is dictation without the push-to-talk. The microphone stays live, your voice becomes text, and the text lands wherever your cursor already is — a terminal, an editor, a chat with your AI.

The gesture costs more than a second. It costs the thought you were holding — the half-formed sentence you were about to say to your terminal, your teammate, your model.

Always removes the gesture. Speaking is the only step. Text is typed and sent for you; hold ⌘ while you talk and the words land without sending.

At your cursor

Speech models hallucinate in the quiet.

Give a speech model a few seconds of silence and it fills the gap with lines it read a million times in subtitles. Always catches them before they reach your cursor.

Say it wrong once. It stays fixed.

Correct a mistranscription and Always learns the fix for good. Add your project’s jargon, and it stops turning “Kubernetes” into “cuber netties”.

From the air to your cursor, in one process.

  1. Microphone

    Captured in shared mode, so other apps keep the device and their volume.

  2. Voice activity

    Silero VAD separates speech from silence in 128 ms chunks.

  3. Speech to text

    Cloud: Groq. Local: Parakeet, Whisper.cpp, Moonshine, SenseVoice or Canary — on-device, offline. Local can back up the cloud automatically.

  4. Filters

    Hallucinations removed, your vocabulary applied, spacing and punctuation tidied.

  5. Your cursor

    Pasted and, unless you held ⌘, sent.

A Rust core inside a Tauri v2 shell. The full map is in ARCHITECTURE.md.

Try quitting it after a week.

Install it, grant the microphone, and go back to work. If your hand reaches for a key before you speak, Always is doing its job.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LivioGama/always/main/scripts/install-always.sh | bash

Or with Homebrew: brew install --cask LivioGama/tap/always

Then grant Microphone, Accessibility and Input Monitoring in System Settings → Privacy & Security. macOS 14 or later.

sudo apt install libwebkit2gtk-4.1-dev
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LivioGama/always/main/scripts/install-always.sh | bash

Installs the DEB, or falls back to the AppImage. Ubuntu 22.04+, Debian 12+, Fedora 38+ with PipeWire, PulseAudio or ALSA. Setup notes in SETUP.md.

Download the MSI installer from GitHub Releases and run it as Administrator, then allow microphone access in Settings → Privacy → Microphone. Windows 10 1909+ or Windows 11.

git clone https://github.com/LivioGama/always && cd always && cargo build --release

Rust toolchain required. Build, test and contribution notes in DEVELOPMENT.md.
