Why Always

AI changed the unit of work from keystrokes to intent.

Keyboards were designed for documents: one character, one gesture, one finished thought. AI development is different. You hold a system in your head, describe the shape of a change, and refine it in conversation.

The bottleneck is no longer typing. It is translating a large, contextual thought into a sequence of small actions. Always removes that translation step. Speak where you are, and your words arrive where the work is already happening.

“Always exists to make the distance between thought and machine so small that the technology disappears.” — Livio, author of Always

The experience

Your hands keep building.
Your voice keeps thinking.

Always is not another window to manage. It is an ambient layer between your intent and the tools you already use.

01

Prompt anywhere without interrupting the current task.

Editors, terminals, chats, browser fields, AI workspaces — wherever your cursor is, that is where your thought goes. No context switch required.

02

Remain in the thought because there is no push-to-talk ritual.

You do not reach for a key before you speak or a window after you speak. The interface stays quiet while your ideas stay continuous.

03

Teach Always the vocabulary that makes your work yours.

Correct a mistranscription once. Add project language when you need it. Your glossary keeps the machine closer to the way you actually think.

Trust is part of the interface

Always-on only works when trust is designed in.

An ambient interface has to earn the right to disappear.

Always makes its listening state visible, keeps your voice profile on your machine, and treats uncertainty as a reason to pause rather than guess.

  • My Voice verificationOptional speaker verification keeps other voices, media, and calls out of your workflow.
  • Pause and conflict safeguardsManual pause, focused-app rules, and microphone conflict handling prevent words from landing in the wrong place.
  • Local transcriptionRun supported models on-device when you want audio to stay on your machine.
  • No telemetryNo account, analytics, or dashboard waiting to turn your work into a product signal.
  • Keychain storageCloud credentials belong in the operating system Keychain, not in config files or logs.
